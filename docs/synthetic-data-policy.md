# Synthetic Data Policy

## Disclaimer
The customer, account, contract, service point, and meter entities in this platform are strictly synthetic entities generated by this project. They do not represent real individuals, and they are not supplied by UK Power Networks (UKPN) or SAP. 

## Separation of Data Types
The architecture strictly separates actual observed energy data from synthetic business configurations:

1. **Real Source-Derived Data**
   - UKPN household identifiers (e.g., `MAC000002`).
   - UKPN energy consumption readings (kWh).
   - UKPN tariff classifications (`stdorToU` -> `stdor_to_u`).

2. **Synthetic Business Data**
   - **Customer**: Mock identifiers, generated profiles. No personally identifiable information (PII) is created or stored (no real names, addresses, phones, emails).
   - **Account**: Simulated contract accounts and statuses.
   - **Contract**: Simulated service contracts linking accounts to premises.
   - **Service Point**: Coarse, simulated regional mapping (e.g., `REGION-LONDON`), explicitly avoiding real street addresses.
   - **Meter**: A synthetic representation of the metering hardware installed at the premise. The actual identifier is derived deterministically from the UKPN household ID.

## Purpose
This synthetic layer exists solely to provide a realistic, relational master-data foundation that allows testing of the billing engine, analytics layers, and frontend dashboard applications, which require a fully-populated SAP-style operational data model to function.
